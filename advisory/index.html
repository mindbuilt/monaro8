What the advisory covers

Help working out what to do, and then help doing it.

  1. Digital strategy

    A digital plan you can actually fund and deliver: 12 to 24 month roadmaps, platform and vendor choices, build-or-buy calls, where AI makes sense and where it’s risky, and briefs a decision-maker can act on.

  2. Product leadership

    An experienced head of product for a while, part-time or interim. Reviews of products and customer journeys, services that work properly on a phone, and product teams set up with clear goals.

  3. Building and proving

    Working prototypes and AI pilots, AI set up in the tools your team already uses, stuck programs back on track, and a clean handover when it’s done.

Where it helps

  • A digital program that’s stalled or drifted and needs an outside view
  • A platform, vendor or build-or-buy decision with a lot riding on it
  • Pressure to use AI, with no clear place to start
  • People dropping out of an online service, application or purchase journey
  • A product or digital team that needs senior leadership for a season
  • An idea that needs a working prototype before anyone funds it
