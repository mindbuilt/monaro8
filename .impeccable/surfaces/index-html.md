Audience: prospective co-founders, partners, investors; secondary Canberra/NSW product leaders. Action: open a venture, or start an enquiry. Proof: four shipped ventures + dated Studio Ledger from real git history. Constraints: no pricing, no fabricated metrics, static Vercel, GA tag kept.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a survey of Cooma. The real Monaro terrain is the ground everything is set on; the ventures are waypoints on it. Refuses the studio-portfolio default (centred headline, card row, logo wall).

OWN-WORLD: Basalt #111312 night relief with lichen minor contours every 20 m and alpine-gold index contours every 100 m; limestone #F4F2ED and stone #EAE6DF editorial sheets ruled in 1px survey hairlines; eucalypt #4A5448 secondary. Inter (optical sizes) set big and tight for display; JetBrains Mono only for coordinates, elevations, dates, ledger data. Map title-block, scale bar, north arrow, graticule ticks as chrome. No radius, no shadows, no gradients except terrain shading.

STORY: Visitor sees a real place and a one-line claim, scrolls across the terrain from plains to foothills, meets four ventures as surveyed waypoints, reads the dated ledger as proof, learns who builds and why here, then routes to contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL contour relief of the 31.6 x 19.7 km Cooma DEM, camera low over the plains looking west. Proposition set large lower-left over the terrain; map title block lower-right (place, coords, datum, scale bar, ASL). Nav top as a map header strip: MONARO8 + live coordinate readout left; Ventures, Thesis, Contact right. Primary action "See the ventures" plus "Start an enquiry" under the proposition.

FORM: Pinned by the founder's brief, which names world and first surface: seed: none (pinned) — "Cartographic Brutalism meets Swiss Editorial"; "The dark basalt hero opens on a 3D wireframe / contour-sliced relief of the Cooma landscape with subtle ambient camera drift and cursor-driven parallax". Signature interaction: scroll flies the camera from plains to foothills; hovering a venture flies the camera to its waypoint and lifts its elevation band.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
