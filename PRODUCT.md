# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, deployed on Vercel (project `monaro8`). No framework rewrite. Three.js and GSAP + ScrollTrigger loaded as ES modules / CDN. Contact form posts to Formspree.

## Users

- **Primary:** prospective co-founders, partners, investors and operators assessing whether MONARO8 builds real businesses, usually arriving from a venture site, LinkedIn or a referral.
- **Secondary:** Canberra / NSW public-sector and enterprise product leaders seeking senior, hands-on product leadership without Big Four overhead (selective inbound advisory).

## Product Purpose

MONARO8 is an independent venture builder based in Cooma, Snowy Monaro, NSW. It finds what wastes people's time and builds software businesses that fix it, using AI tools so a small outfit can build what used to take a big team and keep prices honest (the founder calls it being a "SaaS killer"). The site proves capability through shipped ventures and a dated build log rather than service promises. A separate /advisory/ page captures inbound search for independent digital advisory in Canberra.

## Positioning

"We find what wastes people’s time and build the fix." (Plainer rewrite of the brief’s "We identify market friction and build the businesses that solve it.") Venture-led, not advisory-led. AI-built, honestly priced software. Proof of work over promises. Rooted in a real place: Cooma (36.2355° S, 149.1257° E), the Monaro plains rising to the Snowy Mountains.

## Product Principles

1. Show shipped work before describing capability.
2. The place is the identity: real coordinates, real terrain, never generic.
3. Advisory is available and findable (its own page), but never leads the site and never shows pricing.
4. Fast and static: the signature effect must never cost a mobile visitor.
