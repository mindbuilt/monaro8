SMEasy

Staff rostering for Australian small businesses, from a blank week to approved hours in Xero Payroll.

The problem
Cafés, restaurants and shops with a handful to a few dozen staff, many of them casual, build the week on paper, spreadsheets and group chats, then key the hours into payroll a second time.
The idea
Rostering for a small team should take minutes on a phone, need no app or password for staff, and hand approved hours straight to payroll, at a flat price per business rather than per user.
How it’s built
Managers roster on a week grid or a day view; staff get a personal link to their shifts and time-off requests. Roster links, PDF and email on publish. Timesheets that freeze on approval. Xero Payroll sync sends approved hours to draft timesheets. Supabase Postgres in Sydney, Stripe billing.
Where it’s at
Live. Stripe billing and Xero Payroll sync are switched on.

ZerraLedger

Scope 3 emissions estimates for small suppliers, built from the records they already keep.

Waypoint

Site

The problem
Mandatory climate reporting applies to larger companies, but their emissions include what they buy. The carbon question lands on small suppliers through tenders and procurement, with no reporting obligation of their own and no budget for a consultant.
The idea
A supplier should be able to turn records it already has into an emissions estimate it can send back, with the workings shown, in an afternoon and without a consultant.
How it’s built
A ledger engine on the National Greenhouse Accounts factors published by DCCEEW. Upload spending records, map the columns, preview the report, pay through Stripe. Each saved period is recalculated on the server and hash-chained to the last; buyers can check a report through a public verify link.
Where it’s at
Early access. Registering interest from suppliers while the upload-to-report flow is built. Reports are documented estimates, not independently assured reporting.

BatteryMe

A real installed price for a home battery, rebate included, from an accredited local installer.

The problem
Households with solar can’t get a straight answer on what a battery will cost them. Estimates are generic averages, and real quotes mean days of phone calls and site visits.
The idea
Price the job off real installer rate cards, apply the federal Cheaper Home Batteries rebate up front, and send the homeowner’s details to one installer they choose: no ring-arounds.
How it’s built
A two-question estimate and a six-step assessment that ends in a live installer price. A matching engine in Postgres across installers, coverage and products. Rebate calculator, battery model pages, and postcode pages built from Clean Energy Regulator solar and battery data.
Where it’s at
Live, taking homeowner assessments and installer sign-ups.
Visit BatteryMe

The problem
Checking a domestic flight usually means a booking reference, an airline login or yet another app, and each airline shows its own flights only.
The idea
One independent board for every domestic flight: live departures, arrivals and gate changes across Qantas, Virgin, Jetstar and Rex, open to anyone.

Zerra

Australia’s carbon and clean energy registers in one place: free, login-free and sourced to the Clean Energy Regulator.

The problem
The Clean Energy Regulator publishes Safeguard Mechanism emissions, carbon credit projects, certificates and renewable projects across separate portals, PDFs and spreadsheets that few people can use.
The idea
A free public interface to that data: clear enough for a first-time visitor, credible enough to cite, with the source and date one click under every figure.
How it’s built
Registers for 320 Safeguard Mechanism facilities, 2,923 carbon credit projects and 978 large-scale renewable projects, Guarantee of Origin certificates, a daily certificate-issuance feed, and 2,811 postcode pages for rooftop solar and batteries. Ingested from CER data on daily, monthly and annual cadences.
Where it’s at
Live, free and ad-free. Run by ZerraLedger Pty Ltd alongside ZerraLedger.
