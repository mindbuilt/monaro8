MONARO8 is an independent digital advisory practice based in the Snowy Monaro region of NSW, working with organisations across regional Australia, Canberra, Sydney and beyond.

It brings together digital strategy, product leadership and practical technology delivery to help organisations solve problems, make better decisions and get important work shipped — without the overhead of a large consulting firm or the narrow remit of a single-service vendor.

  • Advisory and strategy engagements
  • Fixed-scope discovery and delivery projects
  • Senior product management and transformation contracts
  • Prototyping and proof-of-concept development
  • Small, specialist teams assembled around a particular need
