Who’s behind it.

MONARO8 is Oliver Moran. Fifteen years leading product teams at Qantas, IAG/NRMA, MetLife and Equifax, then Founder in Residence at Antler Singapore, and EnergyLab. Recently elected President of the Cooma Chamber of Commerce.

Cooma’s a working town. People here run small businesses, work trades and drive a long way for most things. They haven’t got time for software that’s overpriced or hard to use, and neither have we.
