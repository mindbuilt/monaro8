An independent digital advisory, based in the Snowy Monaro.

MONARO8 is a small, capable digital practice. It sets direction and then helps deliver it, with the experience of a large organisation and the pace of a small one.

Oliver Moran, Principal

I've spent 15 years building digital products and platforms for Qantas, IAG/NRMA, MetLife and Equifax. I ran travel insurance online for both Qantas and NRMA, looked after Qantas Business Rewards, and built products for small and medium businesses at Equifax. My work has centred on federated platforms, API-first delivery and large-scale transformation.

Since going independent I've designed and shipped my own products, including BatteryMe, AllFlights and Founders Cut. I'm an alumnus of Antler Singapore (Founder in Residence) and EnergyLab, and hold a BA in Informatics from the University of Sydney.

I moved to the Monaro by choice. I work with organisations here, in Canberra, in Sydney and across Australia.
